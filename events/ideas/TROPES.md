# Genre reference

What the second hundred scenarios were drawn from, so they aren't seeded by our own recent discussion. For each
genre: films that define it, its tropes, its enemies, how fights go, and its set pieces. Scenarios borrow tropes,
never plots.

## Slasher
- **Films:** Halloween, Friday the 13th, A Nightmare on Elm Street, Scream, The Texas Chain Saw Massacre, Black Christmas, Candyman, Final Destination, You're Next, Happy Death Day.
- **Tropes:** an isolated group, a masked killer with a signature weapon, the final girl, the killer who won't stay down, a legend told at the campfire, phones that don't work, a holiday or anniversary.
- **Enemies:** one unstoppable killer; sometimes a family of killers; a dream killer; death itself as traps.
- **Fights:** you can't win until the end; hiding, running, improvised weapons, traps turned back on the killer, a final showdown.
- **Set pieces:** hiding in a closet, the chase through the woods, the false kill, the body reveal, the last stand in a house.

## Supernatural and haunting
- **Films:** The Exorcist, Poltergeist, The Conjuring, Insidious, The Ring, Sinister, Hereditary, The Others, Paranormal Activity, It Follows, The Babadook.
- **Tropes:** a curse with rules, the house that wants you, possession, the medium, research in old records, the ritual to end it, the thing that follows slowly but always.
- **Enemies:** ghosts, demons, the possessed, cursed objects, a follower that only one person can see.
- **Fights:** mostly not fights; rules, wards, rituals, light and sound; brief possessed attackers.
- **Set pieces:** the séance, the hidden room, the attic, the exorcism, the TV static.

## Folk horror and cults
- **Films:** The Wicker Man, Midsommar, The Witch, Children of the Corn, Kill List, Apostle, The Ritual.
- **Tropes:** the outsider in a closed community, the festival, the sacrifice, the forest god, everyone is in on it.
- **Enemies:** cultists, a forest deity, masked villagers, animal-headed figures.
- **Fights:** escape and infiltration; a mob; the god at the end.
- **Set pieces:** the festival dance, the burning effigy, the maze, the ritual circle.

## Zombie and infection
- **Films:** Night of the Living Dead, Dawn of the Dead, 28 Days Later, Train to Busan, World War Z, [REC], Shaun of the Dead, The Girl with All the Gifts, The Crazies, Rabid.
- **Tropes:** the bite, the turn, the mall, the barricade, the hidden bite, the military that makes it worse, the safe zone that isn't, the cure.
- **Enemies:** slow and fast zombies, specials (bloated, armoured, screamers, big ones), infected animals, raiders, the military.
- **Fights:** constant; melee, guns with scarce ammo, noise management, choke points, vehicles through crowds.
- **Set pieces:** the barricade breaking, the horde in the street, the train car, the rooftop rescue, the hospital.

## Monster and creature feature
- **Films:** Alien, The Thing, Predator, Tremors, The Descent, Pitch Black, The Mist, A Quiet Place, Slither, The Host, Feast, Leviathan, Deep Rising, Pumpkinhead, Dog Soldiers.
- **Tropes:** the thing in the dark, the base under siege, learning its weakness, the hunter hunted, a nest and a queen, the sacrifice to save the others.
- **Enemies:** a single apex hunter; a brood (small fast ones, a big one, a queen); burrowers; things that hunt by sound or heat; parasites.
- **Fights:** hit-and-run, traps, exploiting a weakness, flamethrowers and improvised explosives, a final boss.
- **Set pieces:** the vent crawl, the motion tracker, the blood test, the cave descent, luring it into a trap, the airlock.

## Animal attack and nature's revenge
- **Films:** Jaws, The Birds, Arachnophobia, Anaconda, Lake Placid, Crawl, Piranha, The Grey, Grizzly Man (documentary), Cujo, Backcountry, The Edge, Rogue, Black Water, The Swarm, Kingdom of the Spiders, Them!, Eight Legged Freaks, Sharknado, Cocaine Bear, Frogs, Squirm, Prey (2022).
- **Tropes:** the authorities won't close the beach, the expert nobody listens to, the town festival, the flood or storm that brings them in, the mother protecting young, a whole species turning.
- **Enemies:** sharks, crocodiles, snakes, spiders, bees, ants, rats, bats, piranhas, bears, wolves, dogs, boars, birds, orcas, killer whales, hippos, swarms; giant or mutant versions.
- **Fights:** survival over victory; fire, poison, traps, high ground, boats and cages, the big gun at the end.
- **Set pieces:** the beach panic, the cage dive, the swarm through the house, the flooded basement, the treehouse siege.

## Kaiju and giant monsters
- **Films:** Godzilla, Gamera, Cloverfield, Pacific Rim, King Kong, Rampage, Shin Godzilla, The Host, Colossal, Monsters, The Beast from 20,000 Fathoms.
- **Tropes:** seen from the ground, the military fails, the parasites it sheds, the scientist with a theory, the monster versus monster, the city as the arena.
- **Enemies:** the giant; its spawn; the military; mechs.
- **Fights:** you never fight it directly; you guide it, lure it, use the city against it, fight its parasites, operate a weapon.
- **Set pieces:** the footstep, the collapsing building, the evacuation across a bridge, the subway shelter, the weapon that has one shot.

## Alien invasion
- **Films:** War of the Worlds, Independence Day, Signs, Invasion of the Body Snatchers, They Live, Attack the Block, Edge of Tomorrow, District 9, Battle: Los Angeles, Skyline, Mars Attacks!, The Day the Earth Stood Still, Arrival, Nope, Fire in the Sky, Village of the Damned.
- **Tropes:** the first sign, the signal, the mothership, the walkers, the hidden infiltrators, the weakness found late, the resistance cell, abduction.
- **Enemies:** alien soldiers, drones, walkers, harvesters, infiltrators, abducted humans turned against you, a hive queen or mind.
- **Fights:** guerrilla; outgunned; stealing their weapons; taking down a walker from below; sabotage.
- **Set pieces:** the beam, the shield dome, the crash site, the hiding in the basement while it searches, the uploading of a virus.

## Science fiction and techno-thriller
- **Films:** The Terminator, RoboCop, Westworld (1973), Ex Machina, Event Horizon, Sunshine, Annihilation, The Andromeda Strain, Videodrome, The Fly, Jurassic Park, Coma, Primer, Moon, Looper, The Cube.
- **Tropes:** the experiment gone wrong, the machine turns, the time loop, the clone, the facility lockdown, the corporate cover-up.
- **Enemies:** robots, drones, security systems, mutants, clones, the AI, corporate mercenaries.
- **Fights:** hacking and power as weapons; EMPs; ranged combat against machines; doors as tools.
- **Set pieces:** the server room, the lockdown, the self-destruct countdown, the reactor.

## Action and siege
- **Films:** Die Hard, Assault on Precinct 13, Rio Bravo, The Raid, Dredd, Under Siege, Speed, Air Force One, Con Air, Mad Max: Fury Road, Escape from New York, The Warriors, Green Room, Hard Rain, Cliffhanger, Lethal Weapon, Commando, John Wick, Taken, Rambo: First Blood.
- **Tropes:** one person against a building of mercenaries, the precinct under siege, the heist that becomes a hostage crisis, the bus that can't slow down, the escape across a hostile city, the lone outsider hunted by the town.
- **Enemies:** mercenaries, gangs, a crime family, corrupt cops, private military, heavies, snipers, a boss with a plan.
- **Fights:** gunfights, melee, ambushes and traps, taking weapons from the dead, vehicles, explosions.
- **Set pieces:** the vent crawl, the elevator shaft, the roof, the gauntlet across the city, the car chase, the standoff.

## Disaster
- **Films:** The Poseidon Adventure, The Towering Inferno, Twister, Dante's Peak, Volcano, Armageddon, Deep Impact, The Day After Tomorrow, San Andreas, 2012, The Impossible, Daylight, Deepwater Horizon, Greenland, Into the Storm, The Wave, Contagion, Outbreak.
- **Tropes:** the expert ignored, the countdown, the group that splits up, the sacrifice, the unexpected second hit.
- **Enemies:** the event itself, plus looters, panicked crowds and people who'll do anything to survive.
- **Fights:** rare; navigation, timing and rescue; occasional human conflict.
- **Set pieces:** the capsized ship climb, the flooded tunnel, the collapsing bridge, the run to high ground.

## War and military horror
- **Films:** Aliens, Dog Soldiers, Overlord, Predator, Outpost, The Bunker, Deathwatch, Saving Private Ryan, Black Hawk Down, Enemy at the Gates, Edge of Tomorrow.
- **Tropes:** the squad, the cut-off unit, the secret experiment behind the lines, the sniper duel, holding a position.
- **Enemies:** soldiers, experiments, creatures, monsters in uniform.
- **Fights:** squad combat, holding ground, artillery and air support as tools.
- **Set pieces:** the last stand, the bridge, the bunker, the extraction.

## Cosmic and weird
- **Films:** The Thing, In the Mouth of Madness, The Mist, Annihilation, Color Out of Space, The Void, The Endless, Prince of Darkness, From Beyond, Event Horizon, The Lighthouse.
- **Tropes:** knowledge that harms, geometry that's wrong, the cult that knows, the town that's changed, time and space bending.
- **Enemies:** tentacled things, transformed people, cultists, things from between.
- **Fights:** losing battles; the goal is closing the door, not winning.
- **Set pieces:** the portal, the library, the transformation, the shimmer.

## Survival thriller
- **Films:** The Grey, Alive, Frozen (2010), Open Water, 127 Hours, The Revenant, Cast Away, Buried, Don't Breathe, Panic Room, Hush, The Strangers, Funny Games, Rear Window, Wait Until Dark.
- **Tropes:** cut off from help, the home invasion, the blind or deaf protagonist, the stalker who's there in the dark, nature itself.
- **Enemies:** intruders, predators, the cold, a single killer.
- **Fights:** improvised; darkness and noise as weapons; a single desperate fight.
- **Set pieces:** the safe room, the lights going out, the climb, the trap.

## Comedy horror and pulp
- **Films:** Gremlins, Tremors, Shaun of the Dead, Attack the Block, Evil Dead 2, Army of Darkness, Critters, Killer Klowns from Outer Space, Ghostbusters, The Mummy (1999), Big Trouble in Little China, Snakes on a Plane, Krampus, Little Shop of Horrors, Lake Placid.
- **Tropes:** the rules (don't get them wet), the town of eccentrics, the chainsaw hand, a lot of small monsters, the absurd weapon.
- **Enemies:** small swarming creatures with a big leader, killer clowns, possessed toys, a giant plant.
- **Fights:** frequent, varied and absurd; home-made weapons; environmental kills.
- **Set pieces:** the department store, the bar siege, the kitchen fight, the final explosion.

## Patterns that make good gameplay

Drawn from all of the above, these are what reliably turn a premise into play:

1. **An enemy ladder.** Common enemies, a special or two with their own counter, and a boss or apex (zombies with specials; the brood and the queen; mercenaries, heavies and the boss).
2. **A weakness to discover.** Sound, light, water, fire, salt, cold, a frequency. Learning it is the story; using it is the gameplay.
3. **A place under siege.** A precinct, a mall, a house, a base. Barricades, supplies, choke points and the breach.
4. **A gauntlet across the map.** Get from here to there through hostile ground: the bus, the train, the city at night.
5. **Hunter and hunted.** One apex threat that stalks, with hiding, noise and traps; the fight is turned around late.
6. **Escalation.** Each act adds an enemy type or takes away a resource.
7. **Improvised weapons and environmental kills.** Fire, explosions, machines, drops, doors, vehicles.
8. **A rule-based threat.** It can't cross water; it only moves when you don't look; it follows one person.
